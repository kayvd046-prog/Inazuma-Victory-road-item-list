    Common questions

    Which shops sell special moves in Victory Road?

    944 special moves are spread over nine sources. The Chronicle Department Store has the most at 266, followed by the VS Store with 237, Magic Moves (Odaiba Branch) with 130, Magic Moves (Arcade Branch) and the Special Training Booth with 115 each, and the Spirit Market with 32. Ten more come from the Legendary Chest and one from a serial code; for 38 of the moves added in the Ares and Orion updates the shop is not yet known. Each store's stock grows as you clear the Chronicle and the main story.

    Where do you get Keshin, Totems and Awakenings?

    All 155 hyper moves come from one place: the Spirit Market. That is 93 Keshin, 56 Totems and 5 Awakenings. They are paid for in Legendary and Tales spirits rather than tokens, so the cost column lists the spirits you need instead of a price.

    What does the Spirit Market sell?

    213 items: every one of the 155 hyper moves, 32 special moves, 17 Bond Town objects, 8 pieces of equipment and the Hero Rank spirits.

    How do you unlock kits and emblems?

    All 273 kits and emblems are battle drops, never purchases. The 125 kits all come from Chronicle Rare Drop Battles. Of the 148 emblems, 136 come from Chronicle Hero Battles and the other 12 from Rare Drop Battles.

    Which shop has the most items?

    The Chronicle Department Store, with 489 of the 2,067 items. Then the VS Store with 383, the Special Training Booth with 230 and the Spirit Market with 213.

    Does this list show item prices?

    Where a guide documents the token price, yes. The community spreadsheet this is built on records the shop for every item but not always its price, so for most equipment the cost column is empty and the stats the item gives are shown instead.

    Sources. Most of this comes from the Inazuma Eleven VR Document v3.06 (community spreadsheet, last updated 2 March 2026) and Endou's kit and emblem unlock sheet, with prices added from Operation Sports and NoobFeed guides, and 189 later items from the kizuna datamined dump of game version 6.00.23.00. The base stats of the 4,739 characters in the set builder come from the Inazuma Eleven VR Wiki (MIT, player database of 24 December 2025), as do the rarity multipliers its team builder uses (Growing +10%, Advanced +20%, Top +30%, Legendary +40%, Hero +67%). The combat stats of a character are not stored anywhere: they follow from the seven base stats (Shoot AT = Kick + Control, KP = Pressure×2 + Physical×3 + Agility×4, and so on), formulas that hold to the decimal for all 436 pieces of equipment in this list.

    What is missing. The spreadsheet lists a shop for each item but not its token price, so the Cost / source column is empty for most equipment; where a guide documents a price, or an item is a battle reward, it sits there. The Stats column holds the stats an item gives — the two base stats first, then the Focus, Scramble, Wall, Shoot and KP values beneath — and the Power, Tension and Duration of a move. You can filter on several stats at once and then sort on whichever one you like, or on their total. The moves, equipment and tactics from the Ares and Orion routes and the updates after them were added from a datamined dump of game version 6.00.23.00 (kizuna, MIT, August 2026). Kits and emblems for those routes are not in that dump, so those are still missing.
